# Vestas — Team Lead Warehouse Operations, Offshore Service Campaign (f/m/d), req. 79277
# One requirement per line, taken from the posting (responsibilities, qualifications, competencies).
Lead and supervise the team of service campaign stockkeepers handling materials, spare parts, tools and consumables for offshore operations
Plan daily workloads, allocate tasks, conduct team briefings and toolbox talks and monitor performance for safe and efficient execution
Ensure the team supports Service Campaign Operations and maintain accurate inventory records in SAP
Attend project meetings, seek alignment from Service Campaign and liaise operations and schedules for the team on vessels and harbours
Coordinate with other team leads when materials arrive in their harbour warehouses and ensure timely storage and warehouse dynamics
Support the offshore operations in different sites throughout North and Central Europe
Support planned and unplanned service events and promote a strong safety culture among stockkeepers
Lead the team to report incidents, improve safety standards and advocate process adherence
Monitor KPIs such as GR on time and YMM not delivered cleaned and drive continuous improvement initiatives
Have ownership of the training matrix and organise requirements for stockkeepers to operate in different ports
Support audits and inspections, help the Supply Chain Operations Manager create structure and develop the offshore operation from a supply chain perspective
Apprenticeship and/or degree from an education in warehouse management or logistics
Warehouse experience from the wind industry will be beneficial
Customs knowledge will earn additional points
Computer knowledge: SharePoint, Word, Excel, Outlook, TMS and ERP system such as SAP or AX
Outstanding communication and team management skills and strong leadership
Time management, planning, scheduling and system-thinking skills
Adherence and compliance with local laws and regulations, able to turn instructions into concrete actions within the team
Knowledge of offshore wind logistics and safety standards
Familiarity with SAP or another ERP system
Problem-solving mindset with a relentless focus on efficiency
Highly organised: collect information, find patterns, structure and organise it and create standards for the team and the whole Supply Chain Operations Offshore
